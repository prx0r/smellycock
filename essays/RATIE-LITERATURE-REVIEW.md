## THE THESIS THE WHOLE BOOK SUPPORTS
> **Recognition (pratyabhijñā) is the felt re-cognition of the self — the return by which
> consciousness, in a felt event (camatkāra), re-cognizes an identity it never really lost
> (freedom). Suffering is misrecognition; transformation is the re-rendering that follows
> the recognition-event.**

The book's arc supplies every element:
- the *felt* (Intro surprise; Ch7 camatkāra; Ch4 experience-not-construction)
- the *re-cognition* (Ch2 memory/synthesis; Ch4 pratyabhijñāpratyaya; Conclusion return)
- the *self* (Ch3 absolute subject; Ch4 asymmetry; Ch5 permanence)
- the *freedom* (Ch3 aiśvarya; Ch6 yogin; Ch7 svātantrya; Conclusion)
- the *post-recognition re-rendering* (Ch6 creation-not-revelation; Ch9 difference as
  deployment)
- the *soteriology* (Intro alienation; Ch7 misrecognition; Conclusion show-not-demonstrate)

## THE CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER SYNTHESIS

### INTRO — Recognition and the problem of identity
- **Thesis-move:** Recognition is a felt surprise (victory + defeat) crossing a
  self-difference; suffering = not-knowing-who-I-am.
- **IPK:** 1.1.1 (awakening recognition); IV 16, 18 (the new way, for all).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "Elle prend toujours… la forme d'une surprise, car nous réalisons
  alors brusquement ce que nous savions jusque-là sans le savoir : que ça, c'est moi."
  (*Recognition always takes the form of a surprise — we realize what we knew without
  knowing it: that* that, *is me.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** pratyabhijñā, ātman, paratva, bheda/abheda, ānanda/camatkāra.

### CH 1 — The Buddhist critique of the Self
- **Thesis-move:** The hardest rival denies the Self; but self-luminosity (svaprakāśa) is
  *shared* — the debate is whether it implies a Self. Recognition is an ordinary felt
  cognition.
- **IPK:** 1.2.1–2 (no Self perceived); 1.2.3–6 (memory argument); svasamvedana.
- **Verbatim to cite:** "la conscience ne s'y vise donc pas objectivement… mais s'y sait
  être sur un mode parfaitement immédiat ou 'non positionnel'." (*Consciousness knows
  itself in a non-positional mode —* Ratié links to Sartre.)
- **IPVV hooks:** svaprakāśa, svasamvedana, pratyabhijñā, vikalpa, ābhāsa, drśyānupalabdhi.

### CH 2 — Cognitive synthesis
- **Thesis-move:** Memory is re-appropriation (never truly lost); vimarśa is latent in
  every perception; the synthesizing self is non-objective ("blind, deaf, mute" cognitions
  cannot unify themselves).
- **IPK:** 1.3.4 (asampramoṣa); 1.3.6 (anusandhāna); 1.5.19 (vimarśa in every perception).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "cette 'synthèse des formes antérieure et postérieure du Je'… par
  laquelle le sujet percevant se saisit à la fois comme la conscience d'où émerge l'objet
  et comme la conscience où il se résorbe." (*The synthesis of the anterior and posterior
  forms of the I.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** anusandhāna/an usandhātṛ, smṛti, vimarśa, saṃskāra.

### CH 3 — The absolute subject (ĪPK 1.3.7) ⚠️ CORRECTED
- **Thesis-move:** The bearer is the one consciousness with the powers jñāna–smṛti–apohana
  (knowledge, memory, exclusion); aiśvarya = svātantrya.
- **IPK:** 1.3.7 (THE kārikā, corrected); 1.3.6 (the lead-in).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "un unique Grand Seigneur… possédant les pouvoirs (śakti) de
  connaissance (jñāna), de mémoire (smṛti) et d'exclusion (apohana)." — *jñānasmṛtyapohanaśaktimān.*
- **IPVV hooks:** maheśvara, jñānaśakti/smṛtiśakti/apohanaśakti, aiśvarya, cittattva.

### CH 4 — The Self, the cognitions, their relation
- **Thesis-move:** The "I" is a cognition of recognition (pratyabhijñāpratyaya);
  recognition is *experience, not construction*; identity = dynamism; the never-lost
  identity.
- **IPK:** 1.3.7 (asymmetry); 1.6.3 (manifestation/modes); pratyabhijñāpratyaya.
- **Verbatim to cite:** "une reconnaissance (pratyabhijñā) qui… n'est pas une pure et
  simple construction abstraite, mais une expérience." (*Recognition is an experience, not
  an abstract construction.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** pratyabhijñāpratyaya, anusandhāna, āśraya, svātantrya, aham/idam.

### CH 5 — Common idealism
- **Thesis-move:** Śaiva and Buddhist idealism share consciousness-fundamental; the shared
  ākāra thesis *implies* a permanent subject; the object is re-cognized as one's own
  luminous self (tādātmya); not-recognizing = root of error.
- **IPK:** 1.5.1–3 (the object must be within/be light); 1.5.3 (sākāra via Dharmakīrti).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "Il y a donc une identité (tādātmya) foncière du reflet et du
  miroir… hors du miroir, le reflet n'est rien." (*The fundamental identity of reflection
  and mirror.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** ākāra, prakāśa/prakāśatā, tādātmya, svaniṣṭhita, aham/idam, anubhava.

### CH 6 — The mirror, the dreamer, and the yogin
- **Thesis-move:** Consciousness CREATES (yogin) not reveals (lamp/mirror); freedom is
  established by self-awareness (svasamvit), not hypothesis.
- **IPK:** 1.5.4–6 (refutations); 1.5.7 (⭐ the yogin kārikā); 1.5.8–9.
- **Verbatim to cite:** "cidātmaiva hi devo 'ntaḥsthitam icchāvaśād bahiḥ / yogīva
  nirupādānam arthajātam prakāśayet." (*Like a yogin, consciousness manifests its objects
  by its free will, without material cause.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** svātantrya/aiśvarya, icchā, nirupādāna, vāsanā, pratibimba, dṛṣṭānta.

### CH 7 — Freedom (svātantrya) ⭐ THE LOAD-BEARING CHAPTER
- **Thesis-move:** Freedom is the essence; vimarśa = freedom + felicity + wonder; identity
  = freedom to become Other; alienation = misrecognition of freedom.
- **IPK:** 1.5.10 (icchā); 1.5.11 (⭐ vimarśa = camatkṛti); 1.5.12–13 (caitanya, freedom as
  supreme Word); 1.5.15 (freedom to become Other).
- **Verbatim to cite (THE passage):** "camatkāra… c'est la divine surprise… dans laquelle
  on s'étonne moins de l'objet lui-même que de la **satisfaction qu'on se découvre éprouver**
  à son égard – une satisfaction que l'on n'attendait pas." (*The satisfaction one
  discovers oneself feeling.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** svātantrya, vimarśa/pratyavamarśa, camatkāra/camatkṛti, ānanda/
  paramānanda/nirvṛti, icchā/jñāna/kriyā, kartṛtva, māyāśakti.

### CH 8 — The other: solipsism or intersubjectivity?
- **Thesis-move:** The other is *recognized* (not inferred) as my own subjectivity outside
  myself; resists objectivation (the non-objective felt); monism founds compassion.
- **IPK:** 1.1.5 (the other is guessed, ūhyate, not inferred); II 4.4/4.9; I 5.10; III 1.9.
- **Verbatim to cite:** "être conscient de l'existence d'autrui, c'est reconnaître hors de
  soi sa propre subjectivité." (*Being conscious of the other's existence is recognizing
  one's own subjectivity outside oneself.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** pratyabhijñā, ūha/ūhana, pramātṛ, svatantra, paratva, pratibimba,
  idantāvimarśāsahiṣṇu.

### CH 9 — The ontological status of difference
- **Thesis-move:** Difference is real yet non-dual — the deployment of the Self's dynamic
  essence, an upāya; the "infinitesimal gap from oneself."
- **IPK:** II 3.13–14 (single subject = bhitti); II 4.17–21 (via pratibimba); II 4.20
  (ontology of the act); I 6.3 (apoha).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "la réalité ultime… est éternelle effectuation de l'écart
  infinitésimal à soi-même d'où jaillit cette différenciation." (*Ultimate reality is the
  eternal effectuation of the infinitesimal gap from oneself.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** bheda/abheda, pratibimba, bhitti, upāya, ātmatā, sphurattā.

### CONCLUSION — Identity, or the freedom to become other
- **Thesis-move:** One solution (freedom); identity = freedom-to-become-other; recognition
  = the return that shows (not demonstrates) the already-present Self.
- **IPK:** APS 13 (only the Self's manifestation exists); II 3.17 (usage of "Lord");
  IV 16, 18 (the new way).
- **Verbatim to cite:** "on ne produit aucune connaissance nouvelle chez cet individu."
  (*One produces no new knowledge.*) + "Le traité… ne démontre pas ; il montre." (*The
  treatise does not demonstrate; it shows.*)
- **IPVV hooks:** pratyabhijñā, moha, paśya, svātantrya, krīḍā, anugraha.
