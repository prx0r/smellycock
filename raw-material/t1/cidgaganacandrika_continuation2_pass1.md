# Cidgaganacandrikā — T1 (the working translation) — the continuation II (the powers and the breaths, vv. 48–70)

*The Krama's cosmology-stotra continues. The opening (vv. 1–28) and the vāk-theory (vv. 29–47) are in `cidgaganacandrika_opening_pass1.md` + `cidgaganacandrika_continuation_pass1.md`. This continuation covers vv. 48–70: the siddha/śakti pentads, the creation's illumination, the consciousness-body's object-dependence, the sevenfold peace, the inertness-argument, the breath-sun and breath-moon (the upward/downward currents), and the guru's order (gurukrama). The stotra's middle body — the Krama's physiology in the praise's form.*

## The powers and the breaths (vv. 48–70)

**48** — brahmaṇaḥ prabhṛti siddhapañcakaṃ bhāratīprabhṛti śaktipañcakam | unmanīṃ smarati yastavākṛtiṃ siddhavartmani jayatyasau janaḥ ||
> From Brahmā and so on, the fivefold accomplished (siddha-pañcaka); from Bhāratī and so on, the fivefold power (śakti-pañcaka); he who remembers your form [as] the Unmanā (the mind-transcendent) — that man triumphs on the path of the accomplished (siddha-vartman).
> *[The two pentads: the five siddhas (Brahmā-headed) and the five powers (Bhāratī-headed); the goddess's form as the unmanā (the mind-transcendent) — the same as the MNP's unmanā (our T1, the fourth's withdrawal). The report's śakti-dossier: the power's pentads.]*

**49** — tvaṃ prakāśayasi yaṃ sa sṛjyate dharmidharmatanurartha īśvare | yaḥ prakāśa iha sa tvayā kṛtaḥ tattadākṛtirūpaiti sṛṣṭitām ||
> Whom you illuminate, he is created — the body of the property-and-property-holder (dharmin-dharma-tanu), the object in the Lord; what is manifest (prakāśa) here, made by you, takes the form of this-and-that — attains creation-ness (sṛṣṭitā).
> *[The illumination as creation: the object's being is its being-lit by the goddess; the manifest thing takes its this-and-that form — the report's prakāśa-dossier (the manifest luminosity). The creation-ness (sṛṣṭitā) as the lit-ness.]*

**50** — tvaṃ hi cittanurato na cittatā svena te viṣayacittatāṃ vinā | śiṣyase yadi vinārthamaṇḍalaṃ sargasīmani kuto'sti cittatā ||
> You, indeed, are the consciousness-body (cittanu) — not consciousness-ness (cittatā) alone; by your own [nature], without the object-consciousness-ness (viṣaya-cittatā) — if you remain without the object-circle (artha-maṇḍala), at the creation's summit (sarga-sīman), where is the consciousness-ness?
> *[The object-dependence of consciousness: the consciousness-ness requires the object's circle — the report's A-thesis's dialectic (the manifestation and its content). The rhetorical question (where is consciousness without the object?) is the verse's point.]*

**51** — vāsanā ca na vicitratāpadaṃ tatprasūstvamiha kevalaiva cet | śaktilakṣaṇajagadbhṛtodarī mānasaindriyakavastusūrasi ||
> And the impression (vāsanā) is not the place of variegation (vicitratā); its birth, you alone here — the belly-holder of the world marked by the power (śakti-lakṣaṇa-jagad-bhṛt-udarī), the mother of the mental, the sensory, the material things (mānasa-indriya-ka-vastu) [X].
> *[[X] — "sūrasi" (the mother? the ...?) is the obscure close; the force: the vāsanā does not generate the world's variegation — the goddess, the power-marked belly-holder, does. The report's śakti-dossier: the power as the world's womb.]*

**52** — sārvakālikamidaṃ jagat tvayā bhāsyate vidhṛtaśabdarūpayā | āntaraṃ janani buddhidarpaṇe bāhyamadyatanamakṣarūpayā ||
> This all-time world (sārvakālika) is illuminated by you — holding the word-form (vidhṛta-śabda-rūpayā); the inner, O mother, in the mirror of the intellect (buddhi-darpaṇa), the outer and the present (bāhya-adyatana), by the sense-form (akṣa-rūpayā).
> *[The two illuminations: the inner (the intellect's mirror) by the word-form, the outer-present by the sense-form — the report's vāc/indriya dossiers (the word and the senses as the goddess's illuminations).]*

**53** — yāvadamba ! puruṣasya śāntatā saptamī sakalabhāvavartinaḥ | tāvadadvayavimarśabhinnatā sārvakālikavimarśabhāvitā ||
> As long as, O mother, the man's peace (śāntatā) — the seventh (saptamī), of him who moves in all beings — so long [there is] the non-dual reflexion's distinction (advaya-vimarśa-bhinnatā), the all-time reflexion's cultivation (sārvakālika-vimarśa-bhāvitā).
> *[The seventh peace: the saptamī — the seventh level of the jñāna-bhūmikā-ladder (the Vasistha's seven — the report's jñāna-bhūmikā connection, the same as the Setubandha's commentary on the Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava's seven levels). The reflexion's cultivation (vimarśa-bhāvanā) as the peace's condition.]*

**54** — amba tajjaḍamuśanti bhedibhiryad guṇaiḥ sphuradapohyate kramāt | cittadākṛtinirākṛtiḥ svato bhāti yā sakṛdasau tvameva sā ||
> O mother, they call that inert (jaḍa) which, flashing, is removed (apohyate) by the differentiating qualities (bhedi-guṇa) in order (kramāt); the form-and-formless (cit-tadā-kṛti-nirākṛti) which shines by itself once (sakṛt) — that is you yourself.
> *[The inertness-argument: the inert is what the differentiating qualities remove; the goddess, the form-formless, shines once by herself. The report's śūnya-dossier: the apophatic form-formless. The krama (in order) — the report's rule: the sequence's removal.]*

**55** — jāḍyamamba ! jagato nisargato yatparāprabhṛti bhāti bhedataḥ | tvaṃ tu jāḍyaparipanthinī smṛtā yā vibhāsi sakṛdarthamaṇḍale ||
> The world's inertia (jāḍya), O mother, by its nature, which appears divided, from the Parā and before (parā-prabhṛti); but you are remembered as the enemy of the inertia (jāḍya-paripanthinī), you who shine once in the object-circle (artha-maṇḍala).
> *[The inertia's removal: the world's inertia appears from the Parā downward in the divided order; the goddess, its enemy, shines once. The "Parā and before" (parā-prabhṛti) — the speech-ladder's top as the inertia's start.]*

**56** — viśvametaditi yā'hamityasau nāmarūpaviraheṇa niścitā | sā tvamamba ! na bibharṣi bhāsyatāṃ bhātmano yadasi dharma īśituḥ ||
> "This universe" — as "this" and as "I" (iti yā 'ham), determined by the absence of name-and-form (nāma-rūpa-viraheṇa) — that is you, O mother! You do not bear the being-illuminated (bhāsyatā) — what you are is the light's own (bhā), the Lord's attribute (dharma).
> *[The "this-I" determination: the universe's this-and-I, freed of name-and-form, is the goddess; she is not the illuminated (bhāsya) but the light (bhā) itself, the Lord's own attribute. The report's prakāśa-vimarśa pair: the light and its reflexion.]*

**57** — bhāvadṛk smūtiviyuktamīśvare śūnyaratnamavalambya pudgalam | nirniruktikamidaṃ jagat sthitaṃ cillayordhvagapathānna tattathā ||
> The being-seer (bhāva-dṛk), the person (pudgala) divorced from memory (smṛti-viyukta), in the Lord, holding the void-jewel (śūnya-ratna); this world stands word-less (nirniruktika) — from the path of the consciousness's upward-going (cit-laya-ūrdhva-ga-patha) it is not so [X].
> *[[X] — the verse's last pāda ("from the consciousness's upward-going path it is not so") is the obscure fork; the force: the person, holding the void-jewel (the apophatic terminus), sees the world word-less. The report's śūnya-dossier: the void as the jewel, not mere absence.]*

**58** — śāntatāṃ bhajadanāśritaṃ padaṃ śaktimātrajagadekasadma yat | nirmalajvalanaśaucavajjagat tatra hi tviṣi nimajjitaṃ tvayā ||
> Resorting to the peace (śāntatā), the unsupported station (anāśrita pada), which is the one true abode of the power-only world (śakti-mātra-jagad-eka-sat-sadma); the world, like the purity of the stainless fire (nirmala-jvalana-śaucavat) — there, indeed, in the radiance (tviṣi), immersed (nimajjita) by you.
> *[The power-only world's abode: the unsupported peace-station; the world immersed in the goddess's radiance as the stainless fire's purity. The report's śakti-dossier: the power-only (śakti-mātra) — the world's substance.]*

**59** — siddhaśaktimayabhātṛbhāsanavyāptibhāva upayukprakāśatām | yo'yamarthanikarastvadantike tvatpramātrabhimukhaṃ tanoṣyasau ||
> The host of objects (artha-nikara) — whose being is the pervasion of the illumination of the accomplished-power-formed light (siddha-śakti-maya-bhātṛ-bhāsana-vyāpti-bhāva), turned to the useful-light-ness (upayuk-prakāśatā) — near you, you expand it facing your knowing (tvat-pramātṛ-abhimukham).
> *[The object-host before the knower: the objects, whose being is the accomplished-power-light's pervasive illumination, expanded facing the goddess's knowing — the report's mātṛ-dossier (the object's being as the light's).]*

**60** — draṣṭṭatā jagati yatprakāśane yaśca niścayavidhau pramātṛtā | grāhakatvamapi niścitagrahe vettyataḥ sa tava vṛttipoṣitaḥ ||
> The seeing-ness (draṣṭṛtā) in the world's illumination, and the knower-ness (pramātṛtā) in the ascertainment (niścaya-vidhi), and even the grasping-ness (grāhakatva) in the firm grasp (niścita-graha) — knowing these, he is nourished by your activity (vṛtti-poṣita).
> *[The three-fold knowing (seeing, ascertaining, grasping) as the goddess's activities that nourish the knower — the report's pramātṛ-dossier: the knower's three operations.]*

**61** — āsthitaḥ sadasadarthamaṇḍalaṃ ghīmano'kṣavapurasmitāmayaḥ | yastrayodaśavidhaḥ svatantratā taddvayo'hamasau tavāṃśakaḥ ||
> Standing on the being-nonbeing object-circle (sad-asad-artha-maṇḍala), of the body of the intellect, mind, senses, the ego-ness (asmitā-maya), who is thirteenfold (trayodaśa-vidha), the freedom (svatantratā) — its twofold "I am that" (tad-dvayo'ham) — he is your part (tvad-aṃśaka).
> *[The thirteenfold: [X] — the five senses + the five action-instruments + manas + buddhi + ahaṅkāra, or the twelve + the ego, is the commentary-less fork; the force: the embodied knower, the freedom's twofold "I," is the goddess's part (aṃśa). The report's svātantrya-dossier: the freedom as the self's own.]*

**62** — tattadarthamayabhogyalakṣaṇānandamūrtirayamuttamaḥ pumān | arpaṇena jagataścidambare bhāti saṃhṛtimayaḥ śive tava ||
> This supreme man (uttama pumān), of the bliss-form marked by the enjoyment of this-and-that object (tattad-artha-maya-bhogya-lakṣaṇa-ānanda-mūrti) — by the offering (arpaṇa) of the world in the sky of consciousness (cid-ambara), he shines, made of retraction (saṃhṛti-maya), O Śiva, yours.
> *[The offered world's retraction: the supreme man, the enjoyment-formed, offering the world in the consciousness-sky, shines as the retraction — the report's saṃhāra-dossier: the withdrawal as the offering's fruit.]*

**63** — aṣṭadhā sphurati yā purī śive sūkṣmabhāvamupagamya laiṅgikam | tajjapauṣṭakṛtacittavṛttayo vāsanāstvadudarasthite paśau ||
> The city (purī) that flashes eightfold, O Śiva, attaining the subtle state, the liṅga's (laiṅgika) — those impressions (vāsanā), made the mind-activities by the japa's strengthening (japa-uṣṭa-kṛta-citta-vṛtti) [X], in the beast (paśu) dwelling in your belly.
> *[[X] — "japauṣṭa" (japa-uṣṭa, "heated by japa"?) is the obscure core; the force: the eightfold subtle city (the pury-aṣṭaka — the same as the MNP's first-city-of-eight, our T1's jñāna-bhūmikā) and the impressions in the beast. The report's kañcuka-dossier: the paśu (the bound) dwelling within.]*

**64** — sargasaṃvṛtiśarīrayordvayorbhakṣyabhakṣakapadasthayostayoḥ | antarevamahamāsthitaḥ śive dhīmano'kṣavapurarpitaṃ tvayā ||
> Of the creation and the veil (sarga-saṃvṛti), the two bodies, standing in the stations of the eaten-and-eater (bhakṣya-bhakṣaka-pada) — between the two, thus I, standing, O Śiva — the body of the intellect-mind-senses offered by you.
> *[The eaten-and-eater: the creation and the veil as the two bodies (the devouring pair); the speaker standing between, the intellect-mind-senses body offered by the goddess. The batch's devouring-image (the ghasmara/consumption) in the middle-position.]*

**65** — rājanāt prakṛtirañjanācca māṃ rājasaṃjñamanu bodhakarmaṇoḥ | ṣaṭ tvayā pṛthagamī pravartakāḥ putrabhāvamadhiropitāḥ śive ||
> From the passion (rājasa), from the nature (prakṛti) and the coloring (añjana), me, named "the passionate" (rājasa-saṃjña), following the knowledge-and-action (bodha-karma) — the six, by you, separately, the activators (pravartaka), raised to the son-state (putra-bhāva), O Śiva [X].
> *[[X] — "the six activators" (the six śaktis? the six instruments?) is the commentary-less count; the force: the passionate self, colored by the nature, is activated by the goddess, raised to the son-state. The report's guṇa-dossier: the passionate (rājasa) as the bound register.]*

**66** — ūrdhvavāhamayabindulakṣaṇaḥ prāṇasūrya iha ciddviṣaṭkalaḥ | tatkalādharatanurgurukrame tvaṃ prakāśatanu siddhamaṇḍalam ||
> The breath-sun (prāṇa-sūrya), marked by the drop made of the upward-going (ūrdhva-vāha-maya-bindu-lakṣaṇa), the consciousness's twelve-phonemed (cit-dvi-ṣaṭ-kala) here; its phonemes the lower body (adharatanu), in the guru's order (gurukrame) — you, the light-body (prakāśa-tanu), the accomplished-circle (siddha-maṇḍala).
> *[The breath-sun: the upward-going current, the twelve-phonemed consciousness (the six-six — the report's saṃvit-cakra); the guru's order (gurukrama) as the sequence's frame. The report's prāṇa-dossier: the breath's currents in the Krama's physiology.]*

**67** — yastvadhovahananādalakṣaṇo'pānasoma iha bhogyavigrahaḥ | tasya ṣoḍaśakalāḥ kṛtāstvayā''nandacakratanavo gurukrame ||
> And he, marked by the nāda of the downward-going (adho-vahana-nāda), the breath-moon (apāna-soma), the enjoyment-body (bhogya-vigraha) here — his sixteen phonemes (ṣoḍaśakalā), made by you, the bliss-wheel's bodies (ānanda-cakra-tanu), in the guru's order.
> *[The breath-moon: the downward-going nāda, the sixteen phonemes as the bliss-wheel's bodies — the same sixteen as the moon's kalās (the Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava's sixteen; the report's visarga-dossier). The sun-and-moon pair as the two breaths.]*

**68** — vāsareśarajanīśayoḥ kalāścaṇḍi yāvadudayaṃ vitanvate | tāvadeva divasakṣapodayastvatkṛtaḥ prakaṭaguptavigrahaḥ ||
> Of the day-lord (vāsareśa, the sun) and the night-lord (rajanīśa, the moon), O Caṇḍi, as long as the phonemes (kalā) extend their rising (udaya) — so long the day-and-night's rising (divasa-kṣapa-udaya), made by you, of the manifest-hidden body (prakaṭa-gupta-vigraha).
> *[The day-night: the sun-moon phonemes' rising as the day-and-night's order, made by the goddess, of the manifest-hidden body. The batch's udaya-word (the "rise") again — the MNP's structural term in the stotra's form.]*

**69** — devi taddvayakalāvimiśraṇāt sāmarasyapadameti yā sthitiḥ | bindunādasamavedyavedakā'ntargatā hi tava mūrtiratra sā ||
> O Devī, the state (sthiti) that attains the equal-taste-station (sāmarasya-pada) by the mixing of the two's phonemes (dvaya-kalā-vimiśraṇa) — the known-and-knower (vedya-vedaka) common to the drop-and-nāda (bindu-nāda-sama) — indeed, your form (mūrti), inner (antargatā), is there.
> *[The equal-taste: the two phoneme-sets' mixing as the sāmarasya (the equal-taste — the report's śūnya/krama dossiers' terminus); the bindu-nāda pair (the same as the MNP's dvitīya-udaya v. 2). The goddess's inner form as the equal-taste.]*

**70** — līḍhabhānuśaśimaṇḍalā sthitirlambhitā pralayavahninā tvayā | prāṇadhāmni daśasaptadhā satī mūrtirāśritagurukramā śive ||
> The state, with the sun-and-moon circle devoured (līḍha-bhānu-śaśi-maṇḍalā), hung (lambhitā) by the fire of dissolution (pralayāgni), by you; in the breath's abode (prāṇa-dhāma), being the ten-sevenfold (daśa-saptadhā) [X] — the form (mūrti), resting in the guru's order (āśrita-gurukramā), O Śiva.
> *[The dissolution: the sun-moon devoured, the state hung by the fire of dissolution; the form in the breath's abode. [X] — "daśasaptadhā" (the ten-sevenfold, seventeen?) is the obscure count; the report's saṃhāra-dossier: the dissolution as the goddess's own. The guru's order (gurukrama) — the text's self-location in the transmission.]*
