## The moonbeam and the forms (vv. 251–280)

**251** — tannabhaḥ śrayasi sarvataḥ paraṃ tvaṃ prakāśaśivacandrikā satī ||
> That sky (nabhaḥ), you resort to (śrayasi), on all sides, the supreme (param); you, the shining-Śiva's-moonbeam (prakāśa-śiva-candrikā), being (satī).
> *[The title-word: the goddess as the moonbeam of the consciousness-sky (cidgagana-candrikā — the text's own name) resorting to the supreme sky. The report's prakāśa-dossier: the light and its moonbeam.]*

**252** — śāntamamba yadabhinnamambaraṃ yat prakāśaghanamapyapohyete | yad dvayobhayapadātirocanād yatra muktanikaro nilīyase ||
> The tranquil (śāntam), O mother, the un-divided (abhinnam) sky (ambaram); which, even the light-dense (prakāśa-ghana), is removed (apohyete); which, by the crossing-beyond of the two's-both-stations (dvayo-bhaya-padātirocanāt); where the released-host (mukta-nikaraḥ) you hide (nilīyase).
> *[The tranquil sky: the un-divided sky, the light-dense removed, the two's-both-stations crossed; where the released host hides — the report's śūnya-dossier: the apophatic sky.]*

**253** — tvaṃ padatritayagarbhitodayaṃ nirvyapohamupaguhya śaṅkaram | aikarasyalasadakṣayaprathaṃ nādamudvamasi sarvataḥ śive ||
> You, the three-stations-pregnant-rising (pada-tritaya-garbhita-udaya), the exclusion-less (nirvyapoha), embracing (upaguhya) the Śaṅkara; the one-taste-shining-imperishable-manifestation (aikarasya-lasad-akṣaya-pratha), the nāda — you emit (udvamasi), everywhere, O Śiva.
> *[The three-stations' nāda: the three-abodes-pregnant rising, the exclusion-less, embracing the Śaṅkara; the one-taste's imperishable nāda emitted. The report's visarga-dossier: the nāda's emission.]*

**254** — ye vidanti tava rūpamīdṛśaṃ ye ca nityakṛtatallayodyamāḥ | eṣa tān prati bhavan sudhāmayo ye'nyathā viṣamayaśca tān prati ||
> Those who know your form of such kind (īdṛśaṃ), and those who are ever the doers of the effort of its dissolution (nitya-kṛta-tad-laya-udyamāḥ) — this [one], for them, becomes (bhavan) the nectar-made (sudhāmayaḥ); those otherwise — the poison-made (viṣamayaḥ), for them.
> *[The nectar/poison: the knowers of the form and the dissolution-efforters get the nectar; the others the poison — the ambivalence of the goddess's form. The report's śakti-dossier: the power's two faces.]*

**255** — yaḥ pramajjayati mūḍhacetasaṃ tārayatyapi sa eṣa dhīmataḥ | yena tadbhavasudhāmbudhiḥ śive yajjuṣāṃ vahati nistaraṅgatām ||
> He who drowns (pramajjayati) the foolish-minded (mūḍha-cetasaṃ), and carries across (tārayati) — this [one], the wise (dhīmataḥ); by which that becoming's-nectar-ocean (bhava-sudhāmbudhiḥ), O Śiva, for the engaged (juṣāṃ), bears the waveless-ness (nistaraṅgatām).
> *[The drowning-and-carrying: the goddess drowning the foolish, carrying the wise; the becoming's-nectar-ocean bearing the waveless-ness (the batch's nistaraṅga — the waveless ocean).]*

**256** — arthapañcakasudhāprapūrite yadbhavāmṛtanidhau sukhaplave | padyatāṃ vahasi saṃvidindirā dhāmavastu tadume vapustava ||
> In the being's-nectar-treasure (bhava-amṛta-nidhau), filled with the five-objects'-nectar (artha-pañcaka-sudhā-prapūrite), the pleasure-boat (sukha-plave); you carry (vahasi) the cognition-Lakṣmī (saṃvid-indirā), going (padyatāṃ) — the abode-thing (dhāma-vastu), O Umā, your body (vapus).
> *[The cognition-Lakṣmī: the five-objects-nectar-filled being's-treasure, the pleasure-boat; the goddess carrying the cognition as the Lakṣmī. The report's saṃvit-dossier: the cognition's wealth.]*

**257** — tatra padmarucivedyamaṇḍale svāduni prakaṭameyivān layam | māyayā'mba puruṣo nimīlitaḥ tvaddhiyonmiṣati vidyayāvṛtaḥ ||
> There, in the lotus-shining-known-circle (padma-ruci-vedya-maṇḍale), the sweet (svāduni), having gone to the manifest-dissolution (prakaṭa-meyivān layam) [X]; by the māyā, O mother, the man (puruṣaḥ) closed (nimīlitaḥ); by your intellect (tvad-dhiyā) he opens (unmiṣati), covered by the vidyā (vidyayāvṛtaḥ).
> *[The man's opening: the lotus-known-circle's sweetness, the manifest-dissolution; the māyā-closed man opening by the goddess's intellect, covered by the vidyā. The unmeṣa/nimeṣa again — the report's māyā/vidyā dossiers.]*

**258** — tattadarthadalaśobhitaṃ jahannidramullasitamamba vārijam | tvadvimarśamakarandanirbharaṃ pīṭhatāṃ vrajati yatra cinmayam ||
> The lotus (vārijam), adorned with this-and-that-object's-petals (tattad-artha-dala-śobhitaṃ), abandoning the sleep (jahad-nidram), shining (ullasitam), O mother; full of the nectar of your reflexion (tvad-vimarśa-makaranda-nirbharaṃ) — the consciousness-made (cinmayam), there, goes to the seat-ness (pīṭhatāṃ).
> *[The reflexion-lotus: the object-petalled lotus (the world as the petals), abandoning the sleep, full of the reflexion's nectar (vimarśa-makaranda), going to the seat-ness. The report's vimarśa-dossier: the reflexion as the nectar.]*

**259** — tvaṃ malatrayavilāpinī śive pūrṇapīṭhamahamātmakaṃ śritā | śuddhabhānutanuradvayaprathānandasārarasavāhinī satām ||
> You, the destroyer of the three stains (mala-traya-vilāpinī), O Śiva; the full-seat (pūrṇa-pīṭha), the great-I-made (maha-ātmakaṃ), resorted (śritā); of the pure-sun-body (śuddha-bhānu-tanu), the non-dual-manifestation's-bliss-essence-juice's-bearer (advaya-prathā-ānanda-sāra-rasa-vāhinī) — of the good (satām).
> *[The three stains' destroyer: the malatraya (the āṇava/kārma/māyīya — the report's mala-dossier), the full-seat great-I; the pure-sun-body, the non-dual-bliss-juice's bearer. The Siddhānta's malas in the Krama's non-dual frame.]*

**260** — satsudhābdhirasabhṛt kalāntarī garbhaṣoḍaśakalābjaviṣṭaram | pretamujjhitagamāgamaṃ śive smerabhūḥ sarasijaṃ tvamāśritā ||
> The true-nectar-ocean's-juice-bearer (sat-sudhā-abdhi-rasa-bhṛt), the phoneme-interior (kalāntarī) [X]; the sixteen-phonemes-lotus-seat (garbha-ṣoḍaśa-kalā-abja-viṣṭaram); the ghost (pretam), the exit-and-entry-left (ujjhita-gamāgamaṃ), O Śiva, the smiling-ground (smera-bhūḥ), the lotus (sarasijam), you have resorted (āśritā).
> *[The sixteen-phoneme lotus: the true-nectar-ocean's juice, the sixteen-kalā lotus-seat (the batch's sixteen); the ghost [X] (the pretam — the abandoned form?), the exit-entry-left, the smiling lotus. [X] — "pretam" is the register's fork (the śava/corpse form?).]*

**261** — niṣkalaṅkamanavekṣitakṣayaṃ yāminīdivasatūlajṛmbhaṇam | svaprakāśavinimagnacijjaḍaṃ candravarṣma yadi caṇḍike samam ||
> The un-stained (niṣkalaṅkam), the un-seen-destruction (anavekṣita-kṣayaṃ), the night-day's-straw-expansion (yāminī-divasatūla-jṛmbhaṇam) [X]; the consciousness-inert (cijjaḍaṃ) immersed in the self-light (svaprakāśa-vinimagna) — the moon-body (candra-varṣma), if, O Caṇḍike, equal (samam).
> *[The moon-body: the un-stained, un-seen-destruction; the night-day's expansion [X]; the consciousness-inert immersed in the self-light — the moon's body. [X] — "yāminīdivasatūla" (the night-day's straw?) is the corrupt image.]*

**262** — ye tu kuṇḍalaśikhākulasthitāḥ ye bhavantyakulabinduśālinaḥ | te vidanti nu tavāmba vaibhavaṃ pūrṇabhānamayaviśvaghasmaram ||
> Those who stand in the kuṇḍala, the śikhā, the kula (kuṇḍala-śikhā-kula-sthitāḥ); those who become the possessors of the akula's-drop (akula-bindu-śālinaḥ) — do they know, O mother, your glory (vaibhavaṃ), the full-light-made world-devourer (pūrṇa-bhāna-maya-viśva-ghasmaram)?
> *[The kuṇḍala/śikhā/kula standers: the circle, the flame, the kula — the phoneme-seats; the akula-drop possessors; the question (do they know the glory?). The report's kula/akula dossiers: the pair's stations.]*

**263** — jyotiṣāmba ! śikhayāpahanyate puṃsi caitasikamuddhataṃ tamaḥ | yatpathaprathanalakṣmapuṅkhitaṃ tattvameva śivaśaktirapyasi ||
> By the flame (śikhayā), O mother of the lights (jyotiṣām), is struck down (apahanyate) in the man (puṃsi) the uplifted mental darkness (caitasikam uddhataṃ tamaḥ); that which is feathered by the path-manifestation's-mark (patha-prathana-lakṣma-puṅkhitaṃ) [X] — that tattva itself, the Śiva-power (śiva-śakti), you are.
> *[The flame's darkness-strike: the lights' mother's flame striking down the mental darkness; the path-marked [X]; the Śiva-power as the tattva. [X] — "pathaprathanalakṣmapuṅkhitaṃ" (feathered by the path's mark?) is the corrupt image.]*

**264** — nihnutoditaśatārkadīdhitiśchannacandraśatalakṣmacandrikam | tvaṃ bibharṣi vapuramba bhātmakaṃ tatpraveśya kuru māmanāmayam ||
> With the risen-hundred-suns'-rays-hidden (nihnuta-udita-śatārka-dīdhiti) [X], the hundred-moons'-marks'-moonbeam-covered (channa-candra-śata-lakṣma-candrikam) — you bear the light-made body (bhātmakaṃ vapuḥ), O mother; entering that (tat-praveśya), make me ail-less (mām anāmayam).
> *[The hundred-suns: the risen-hundred-suns hidden, the hundred-moons' moonbeam covered; the light-made body borne; the entering (praveśya) making the speaker ail-less — the prayer's voice.]*

**265** — tvaṃ kulākhyaśaśibhānupāvake bhrājamānakaraṇātmanāyike | saṃsmṛtāsi municittamaṇḍape adya me kuru tavāṅghrisaṃśrayam ||
> You, in the kula-named moon-sun-fire (kula-ākhya-śaśi-bhānu-pāvake), the blazing-instruments' mistress (bhrājamāna-karaṇa-ātma-nāyike); remembered (saṃsmṛtā) in the seers'-minds'-pavilion (muni-citta-maṇḍape) — today, for me, make the resort of your feet (tvad-aṅghri-saṃśrayam).
> *[The feet's resort: the kula's moon-sun-fire (the batch's agnīṣoma), the instruments' mistress; remembered in the seers' minds; the prayer for the feet's resort.]*

**266** — pañcavāhagata śaktilakṣaṇaistvanmukhairakhilamarthamaṇḍalam | gṛhṇatī mayi niveśya caṇḍike yā karoṣi sahajaṃ tavādhvaram ||
> The whole object-circle (akhilam arthamaṇḍalam), with the five-power-gone (pañca-vāha-gata), the power-marked (śakti-lakṣaṇaiḥ), by your faces (tvan-mukhaiḥ); grasping (gṛhṇatī), placing in me (mayi niveśya), O Caṇḍike — which you make, the innate sacrifice (sahajaṃ tavādhvaram).
> *[The innate sacrifice: the five-power object-circle grasped by the goddess's faces, placed in the speaker; the innate sacrifice (sahaja-adhvara — the Kaula's sahaja, the batch's term). The report's śakti-dossier: the power's faces.]*

**267** — tvatpadasmṛtividhūtakalmaṣaṃ sarvataḥ sphuritapūrṇabhāghanam | siddhacakramiha modate śive pūrṇimāpyahaha me na tāpahā ||
> The accomplished-circle (siddha-cakra), its stains removed by the memory of your station (tvat-pada-smṛti-vidhūta-kalmaṣaṃ), everywhere, the flashing-full-light-dense (sphurita-pūrṇa-bhā-ghanam), here delights (modate), O Śiva; even the full-moon (pūrṇimā) — alas (ahaha) — is not the heat-remover (tāpahā) for me.
> *[The accomplished-circle's delight: the stains removed by the station's memory; the full-light-dense; the full-moon's inadequacy (the heat-remover) — the praise's hyperbole. The report's siddha-dossier: the accomplished-circle.]*

**268** — bhūtabhāvibhavadarthagarbhitaṃ kālaghasmaramupāsyate vapuḥ | kālakarṣiṇi vahanti yoginaḥ kṛṣṇataḥ prabhṛti viśvarūpatām ||
> The body (vapuḥ), pregnant with the past-future-becoming-objects (bhūta-bhāvi-bhavad-artha-garbhitaṃ), the time-devourer (kāla-ghasmaram), is worshipped (upāsyate); O Kālakarṣiṇi, the yogins bear (vahanti), from the black (kṛṣṇataḥ) on, the all-form-ness (viśva-rūpatām).
> *[The Kālakarṣiṇī's all-forms: the time-devouring body (the batch's shared Kālasaṃkarṣaṇī), worshipped; the yogins bearing the all-form-ness from the black (the kṛṣṇa — the goddess's black form?) on. The report's kālī-dossier.]*

**269** — sparśarūparasagandharūpajaṃ śabdacidvalasabhā vibhindatī | vṛtti ca kramakṛtāḥ ṣaḍadhvano deśakālamayamūrticakragā ||
> The touch, the form, the taste, the smell-form-born (sparśa-rūpa-rasa-gandha-rūpa-ja), the sound-consciousness's-dance-company (śabda-cid-valasabhā) [X], dividing (vibhindatī); the activity (vṛtti) and, made by the order (krama-kṛtāḥ), the six courses (ṣaḍadhvanaḥ) — going in the space-time-made-form-circle (deśa-kāla-maya-mūrti-cakra-gā).
