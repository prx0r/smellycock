## The closing and the colophon (vv. 281–312)

**281** — cinmayaṃ paramarūpalakṣaṇaṃ rūpamantaravabhāti tadbabahiḥ ||
> The consciousness-made (cinmayaṃ), marked by the supreme form (parama-rūpa-lakṣaṇaṃ), the form (rūpam) shines within (antar avabhāti); that, outside (tad bahiḥ).
> *[The consciousness-form: the supreme-form-marked form shining within and without — the report's prakāśa-dossier: the form's non-duality.]*

**282** — strīti nāma tava kāpi kalpanā strītirūpamapi tādṛśī ca dhīḥ | vastutastvamasi viśvavigrahā tatparā ca citireva śāṅkarī ||
> "Woman" — such a name of yours, some imagination (kāpi kalpanā); the woman-form, even, such a thought (tādṛśī dhīḥ); in truth, you are the world-bodied (viśva-vigrahā), the supreme (tatparā), indeed, the consciousness-form (citireva), the Śāṅkarī.
> *[The beyond-the-genders: the "woman" as a name only, the world-bodied consciousness-form, the Śāṅkarī — the report's ātma-dossier: the formless beyond the name.]*

**283** — tvaṃ caturthapadavartinī śive yaddvitīyamapi tatpadaṃ sthitā | yo'pi pañcamapadāśritaḥ śive yastṛtīyamapi tatpadaṃ sthitaḥ ||
> You, dwelling in the fourth-station (caturtha-pada-vartinī), O Śiva; what is even the second (dvitīyam) — that station you stand; he who is even resorted in the fifth (pañcama-padāśritaḥ), O Śiva; what is even the third (tṛtīyam) — that station [he] stands.
> *[The stations' transcendence: the fourth dwelling in the second, the fifth in the third — the stations threaded by the beyond. The report's jñāna-bhūmikā: the levels' mutual-pervasion.]*

**284** — yatpadaṃ prathametaduttare tatpare tadapi turyagāminī | tvaṃ turīyatanurāhṛtatrikā pañcamaṃ viśasi pūritatrikam ||
> The station which is the first, this, in the higher (uttare); that, the supreme (tat-para); that, even, the fourth-going (turīya-gāminī); you, the fourth-body (turīya-tanu), the triad-brought (āhṛta-trikā), enter the fifth (pañcamaṃ viśasi), the filled-triad (pūrita-trikam).
> *[The fourth's fifth: the fourth-body entering the fifth (the beyond-the-fourth — the same turīyātīta as the MNP's jñāna-bhūmikās, our T1). The report's jñāna-bhūmikā: the four-and-beyond.]*

**285** — ambareśvari nirākṛtestava vyajyate nikhilamākṛtitvataḥ | sarvadā sphurasi yeyamadvayānandacitprasarapīṭhavartinī ||
> O sky-lady (ambareśvari), of your formless (nirākṛteḥ), all is shown (nikhilam vyajyate) by the form-ness (ākṛtitvataḥ); always you flash (sarvadā sphurasi), this [one], dwelling in the non-dual-bliss-consciousness-outflow's-seat (advaya-ānanda-cit-prasara-pīṭha-vartinī).
> *[The sky-lady's formless: the formless shown through all the forms; the non-dual-bliss-consciousness-outflow's seat (the prasara — the batch's saṃvit-prasara, the MNP's sequence-engine).]*

**286** — na śrutirna ca giro na cāgamāḥ khaṇḍavastu viniveśitāśayāḥ | tvāṃ nayanti parameśi te makhā yāgayogavidhayaśca nedṛśīm ||
> Not the śruti, nor the speech (giro), nor the āgamas (āgamāḥ), of the divided-thing-placed-minds (khaṇḍa-vastu-viniveśita-āśayāḥ); they do not lead you (tvāṃ nayanti), O supreme lady (parameśi), the rites (makhāḥ), the yāga-yoga-procedures (yāga-yoga-vidhayaḥ), nor the such-one (nedṛśīm).
> *[The scriptures' inadequacy: the śruti, the speech, the āgamas, the rites, the procedures — none leads the such-one (the goddess beyond the means). The report's anākhya-dossier: the un-nameable beyond the means.]*

**287** — nityatādiguṇalakṣite pade jñānayogamukhakalpanāpi te | amba na tvadanubhūtidāyinī tvallayaikagurusaṃśrayaṃ vinā ||
> In the station marked by the eternity-and-the-rest (nityatā-ādi-guṇa-lakṣite pade), even the imagination of the knowledge-yoga's-face (jñāna-yoga-mukha-kalpanā), of yours, O mother, is not the giver of your experience (tvad-anubhūti-dāyinī), without the resort of the single guru with your dissolution (tvad-laya-eka-guru-saṃśrayaṃ).
> *[The guru's necessity: the knowledge-yoga's face not giving the experience without the guru-resort — the Krama's guru-principle (the same as the Yoginīhṛdaya's karṇāt-karṇa transmission, our T1).]*

**288** — vyomabhāvaviniviṣṭacetasaḥ prātibhaṃ jagadavekṣya ninditāḥ | te tvamāviśasi yānanārataṃ tvannayena guruṇāmba ! vīkṣitān ||
> The minds-placed-in-the-sky-being (vyoma-bhāva-viniviṣṭa-cetasaḥ), seeing the intuitional world (prātibhaṃ jagad), the censured (ninditāḥ) — those (te) you enter (āviśasi), going unceasingly (anāratam), by your method (tvannayena), O mother, seen by the guru (guruṇā vīkṣitān).
> *[The guru-seen: the sky-placed minds, the intuitional world, the censured, entered by the goddess, seen by the guru. The report's pratibhā-dossier: the intuitional.]*

**289** — nityatā tvayi vibhutvapūrṇate dṛkkṛye svarasagocare ca ye | tāni tāni viyatīva śobhane rāgavedasakalāśca bhāntyatha ||
> The eternity (nityatā), in you, the lordship-fullness (vibhutva-pūrṇate), the seeing-action (dṛk-kṛye), the self-taste's-range (svarasa-gocare), and those [others]; those, those, like the sky (viyat iva), O beautiful one, the passion and the Vedas' phonemes (rāga-veda-sakalāḥ), shine, then.
> *[The attributes' shining: the eternity, the fullness, the seeing-action, the self-taste, the passion, the Vedic phonemes — all shining in the goddess like the sky. The report's guṇa-dossier: the attributes' play.]*

**290** — vyomavṛttighaṭitaikya ullasatyadvayo janani ! devatāgaṇaḥ | tāvakodyamapade'pi sarvago drāgvilāpanapadaṃ vināpi te ||
> The deity-host (devatāgaṇaḥ), the sky-activity's-made-unity (vyoma-vṛtti-ghaṭita-ekya), the non-dual (advayaḥ), shines (ullasati), O mother; in your-effort's-station (tāvakodyama-pade), even, the all-going (sarvagaḥ), at once (drāk), even without the dissolution-station (vilāpana-padaṃ vinā).
> *[The deity-host: the sky-activity's unity, the non-dual, shining in the goddess's effort-station. The report's devatā-dossier: the host's unity.]*

**291** — itthamamba tava vṛttipañcakasyodayo'dbhutavibhāgasaṃbhramaḥ | sarvadā sphurati sarvataḥ paraṃ khaṇḍitetaranijasvarūpabhāk ||
> Thus, O mother, of your five-activities (vṛtti-pañcaka), the rising (udaya), the wonderful-division's-confusion (adbhuta-vibhāga-saṃbhramaḥ), always flashes (sarvadā sphurati), everywhere, the supreme (param); the divided-other (khaṇḍita-itara), one's-own-form's partaker (nija-svarūpa-bhāk).
> *[The five activities' rising: the wonderful division flashing always, the divided-other sharing its own form. The report's vṛtti-dossier: the activities' play.]*

**292** — khaṇḍitetarasamastagarbhago yastripañcavisarāśrayo'kramaḥ | devatākrama udetyanārataṃ tasya hi tvamasi devajanmabhūḥ ||
> He who, the divided-other-all-womb-gone (khaṇḍita-itara-samasta-garbhagaḥ), the fifteen-outflow's-support (tri-pañca-visara-āśrayaḥ), the orderless (akramaḥ); the deity-order (devatā-krama) rises (udeti), unceasingly; of that, indeed, you are the deities' birth-ground (deva-janma-bhūḥ).
> *[The deity-order: the orderless, the fifteen-outflow's support, rising unceasingly; the goddess as the deities' birth-ground. The report's krama-dossier: the deity-order's beyond.]*

**293** — divyavāhavibhavodarasthito raśmimaṇḍalasamudgamāspadam | avyayastava navārṇavigrahe saṃhṛtikrama udetyakalpitaḥ ||
> Dwelling in the womb of the divine-current's-glory (divya-vāha-vibhava-udara-sthitaḥ), the ground of the rays-circle's-emergence (raśmi-maṇḍala-samudgama-āspadam); the imperishable (avyayaḥ), in your nine-phoneme-form (nava-arṇa-vigrahe), the retraction-order (saṃhṛti-krama) rises (udeti), un-imagined (akalpitaḥ).
> *[The nine-phoneme form: the divine-current's womb, the rays-circle's ground; the retraction-order rising in the navārṇa (the nine-letter mantra — the batch's navārṇa, the Kaula's). The report's saṃhāra/mantra dossiers.]*

**294** — lakṣyaśūnyasahajāśramodayārambhapūrṇanijakoṭyabhedinī | raśmicakratanuraśmitojjhitā tvaṃ ca gauri ! varapuṇyavāsanā ||
> The target-void (lakṣya-śūnya), the innate (sahaja), the stages (āśrama) [X], the rising (udaya), the beginning (ārambha), the full (pūrṇa), the own's-crore (nija-koṭi), the undivided (abhedinī); the rays-circle-body (raśmi-cakra-tanu), the rays-left (raśmi-ujjhitā) [X] — you, O Gaurī, the best-merit's-impression (vara-puṇya-vāsanā).
> *[The target-void: the sahaja (the innate — the batch's Kaula term), the stages, the rising-beginning-full; the rays-circle-body; the Gaurī, the best-merit's impression. [X] — "āśramāḥ" (the stages) and "raśmitojjhitā" (the rays-left) are the readings' forks.]*

**295** — adhvabhirdhvanivimiśraṇakramamabjabījamaniketanaṃ varam | kalpanaikyabahubhedamadvayaṃ mantrapīṭhamasi kālyanargalam ||
> By the courses (adhvabhiḥ), the sound-mixture's-order (dhvani-vimiśraṇa-kramam); the lotus-seed (abja-bīja), the without-abode (aniketanaṃ), the best (varam); the imagination-unity-many-divisions (kalpana-ekya-bahu-bhedam), the non-dual (advayaṃ) — you are the mantra-seat (mantra-pīṭham), the time-able's-unbarred (kālya-anargalam).
> *[The mantra-seat: the adhvan-sound mixture's order, the lotus-seed, the without-abode; the non-dual imagination-unity; the mantra-seat, the time-able's unbarred. The report's adhvan/mantra dossiers: the seat's nature.]*

**296** — spandamamba paripūrṇamapyabhūḥ spandamu~jjhitapadaṃ nirāvṛti | vyomarūpamakhilakramāspadam pīṭhamāvadhikavidyayāmbayā ||
> The pulse (spandam), O mother, the full (paripūrṇam), even, you became (abhūḥ); the pulse, the station-left (ujjhita-padam), the un-covered (nirāvṛti); the sky-form (vyoma-rūpa), the all-order's-ground (akhila-kramāspadam) — the seat (pīṭham), by the limit-knowledge (āvadhikavidyayā), O mother.
> *[The pulse-seat: the full pulse, the station-left, the un-covered; the sky-form, the all-order's ground; the seat by the limit-knowledge. The report's spanda/krama dossiers: the pulse as the seat's ground.]*

**297** — aṣṭayonidalapatrapūruṣaṃ tvantavṛttidalaśaktipeṭikā | tvaṃ patiśca tava liṅgavigraho rudrasiddhamunivṛndavandite ! ||
> The eight-wombs'-petals'-leaves'-man (aṣṭa-yoni-dala-patra-pūruṣaṃ) [X]; your-activities'-petals'-power's-casket (tvad-antavṛtti-dala-śakti-peṭikā); you, and your lord (patiḥ), the liṅga-body (liṅga-vigrahaḥ) — O worshipped by the Rudra-siddha-seers' host (rudra-siddha-muni-vṛnda-vandite)!
> *[The liṅga-body: the eight-wombs' petals' man (the yoni-liṅga pair — the batch's Kākacaṇḍeśvarīmata's own yoni-doctrine); the activities' petals' power-casket; the lord's liṅga-body. [X] — "aṣṭayonidalapatrapūruṣaṃ" is the compound's fork.]*

> What, from the creation on, the fourfold (catuṣṭayaṃ); what, born of the light-like-form's-host (prakāśa-nibha-mūrti-vṛnda-ja); and what, the divided-station (khaṇḍa-padam), O mother, the seat-gone (pīṭhagaṃ) — give the nine-wheel (cakra-navakaṃ), praised (nutaṃ), of yours.

**299** — tvaṃ navārṇamanucakravigrahā svodarārpitasamastadevatā | devi saptadaśavarṇabheditā tvaṃ prakāśaśivacandragāminī ||
