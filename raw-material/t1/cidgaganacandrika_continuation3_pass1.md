# Cidgaganacandrikā — T1 (the working translation) — the continuation III (the pīṭha and the goers, vv. 71–95)

*The Krama's cosmology-stotra continues. The vāk-theory (vv. 29–47) and the powers-and-breaths (vv. 48–70) are in the two earlier continuations. This continuation covers vv. 71–95 — the pīṭha-section: the turīya-threaded states, the fourth station, the speech-quadruple, the seat-circle (pīṭha-cakra), the burning-ground (śmaśāna), the field-lord (kṣetrapāla), the four goers (khecarī, bhūcarī, dikcarī, gocarī), and the Kālakarṣiṇī — the Krama's internalized geography and physiology at its most complete.*

### Provenance
- **Author:** Śrīvatsa (the Krama's Ācārya)
- **Text:** Cidgaganacandrikā — the continuation: the pīṭha-section
- **Digital source:** M00014 (1937 Calcutta edition) — no commentary
- **Collation:** single-edition; the batch-siblings' citations (the Yoginīhṛdaya's Dīpikā quotes the khecaratā-verse) anchor the khecarī-doctrine
- **Anchors:** none on disk; the MNP's pīṭha-cakra (our T1, the caturtha udaya) is the sibling-parallel — the same seats and the same goddesses

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** the Krama's mature stotra-layer (≈ 12th–13th c.); the edition 1937
- **PLACE/PATH:** Kashmir
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** maṅgala-stotra in high literary verse — the pīṭha-geography and the sensory-goers in the praise's form
- **FRAME:** the continuation's arc: the fourth-threaded three states (71) → the fourth station (72) → the cāra/rāva/caru worship (73) → the speech-quadruple (74) → the power-circle's freed man (75) → the equal-tasted Śiva's seat (76–77) → the seat-circle's mother (78) → the phoneme-host's hero (79) → the fire-sun-moon kalā (80) → the sixteenfold seat (81) → the burning-ground (82) → the field-lord (83) → the guru-order from khecarī to Śākinī (84–85) → the mind's darkness and the sacrifice (86) → the fivefold bearer (87–88) → the void-abode (89) → the four goers (khecarī, bhūcarī, dikcarī, gocarī, 90–93) → the Brahmā-bodied kalā (94) → the Kālakarṣiṇī (95). The MNP's caturtha-udaya parallel (the same pīṭha-cakra) is the batch's cross-check.

## The pīṭha and the goers (vv. 71–95)

**71** — turyacitpadamayuḥ prasāritāḥ suptitaḥ prabhṛti dhāmasu triṣu ||
> [The light,] of the measure of the fourth-consciousness's station (turīya-cit-pada-māyuḥ), extended, from the sleep and before, in the three abodes (dhāma-traya).
> *[The turīya threading the three states (waking/dream/sleep) — the report's jñāna-bhūmikā connection (the same four-and-beyond as the MNP's and the ŚS 1.7's Fourth). The first pāda is the ellipsis of the verse's opening; the force: the three abodes lit by the fourth's measure.]*

**72** — meyamātṛmitilakṣaṇaṃ kulaṃ prāntato vrajati yatra viśramam | tatpadaṃ tava turīyamambike yanmahadbhiranupākhyamiṣyate ||
> The kula, marked by "the known, the mother, the measure" (meya-mātṛ-miti), goes to its rest at the end (prāntataḥ) — that station, your fourth (turīya), O Ambikā, which the great call the un-nameable (anupākhya).
> *[The fourth station: the kula's rest, the turīya, the un-nameable (anupākhya) — the report's kula-dossier (the manifest pole's rest) and the anākhya-dossier (the un-nameable fourth, the Krama's terminus). The known/mother/measure triad (the same mātṛ-meya-māna as the MNP's tṛtīya udaya).]*

**73** — cārarāvacarubhirvibheditairmudrayā ca yadupāsanaṃ tava | tadvaśena bhajate paramparāṃ tāvakī kramatayā sphuṭākṛtiḥ ||
> The worship of you, divided by the cāra, the rāva, the caru [X], and the mudrā — by that [worship] your lineage (paramparā) attains, by the order (kramatayā), the clear-form (sphuṭākṛti).
> *[[X] — the cāra/rāva/caru triad (the motion, the sound, the offering) is the worship's fork; the rāva (the sound) is the MNP's rāva-krama (the sound-order, our T1's pañcama-udaya) — the batch's shared sound-lexicon. The order (kramatayā) as the worship's frame.]*

**74** — maṅgale tvamasi vākcatuṣṭaye yā parā sphurati mūrtimaṇḍale | sā prakāśa-sukha-vṛndacakragā nādabindulipimūrtirijyase ||
> In the auspicious [opening] (maṅgale), you are in the speech-quadruple (vāk-catuṣṭaye); she who flashes in the form-circle (mūrti-maṇḍala), the supreme (parā) — she, going in the circle of the light-and-bliss-host (prakāśa-sukha-vṛnda-cakra), the nāda-bindu-letter-bodied (nāda-bindu-lipi-mūrti), is worshipped.
> *[The speech-quadruple (the four speeches — the report's vāc-dossier) as the goddess's maṅgala; the nāda-bindu-letter body — the same as the MNP's dvitīya-udaya v. 2 and the Cidgaganacandrikā's own v. 31 (the phonemic triad).]*

**75** — ādyacitprasaramūrtirambike pītatāṃ vrajati yatkṛtāspadā | akṣayaṃ śivamupāsya tajjanaḥ śakticakrajanitaḥ pramucyate ||
> The first-consciousness-outflow's form (ādi-cit-prasara-mūrti), O Ambikā, which, made the seat (kṛtāspadā), goes to the seat-ness (pīṭhatā) [X]; worshipping the imperishable Śiva, that man, born of the power-circle (śakti-cakra-janita), is freed (pramucyate).
> *[[X] — "pītatāṃ" (the reading "pīṭhatāṃ," the seat-ness, is the fork); the force: the first-outflow's form becomes the seat; the power-circle-born man, worshipping the imperishable Śiva, is freed. The report's saṃvit-dossier: the first-outflow (prasara) — the Krama's sequence-engine.]*

**76** — amba ! śaktivapuṣā tvayonmiṣadrūpayā samarasaḥ śivo yadā | yattadollasati vīryamūrjitaṃ pīṭha eṣa hi mahāṃstvadutthitaḥ ||
> O mother, when Śiva, equal-tasted (samarasa), by your power-body (śakti-vapus), the flashing-form (unmiṣad-rūpayā) — that which flashes, the strong vigor (vīrya-ūrjita) — this great seat (mahā-pīṭha) indeed, risen from you.
> *[The seat's rise: the equal-tasted Śiva and the flashing power as the great seat's parents — the report's sāmarasya (the equal-taste) and the pīṭha's generation.]*

**77** — yā śive sphuraṇaśaktirakṣayā kṣmādipañcakaniviṣṭabhātanuḥ | sā mahad bhavati vīryamagrimaṃ yanmayī tvamasi pīṭhacakrasūḥ ||
> She who, O Śiva, [is] the flashing-power (sphuraṇa-śakti), imperishable (akṣayā), whose being-body is seated in the five, earth-and-the-rest (kṣmādi-pañcaka-niviṣṭa-bhāta-tanu) — she becomes the great, the primeval vigor (vīryam agrimaṃ) — made of which you are the mother of the seat-circle (pīṭha-cakra-sū).
> *[The power's seat: the flashing-power, whose body is the five elements (the pīṭha's five — the same as the MNP's caturtha-udaya's five), as the primeval vigor; the goddess as the seat-circle's mother (pīṭha-cakra-sū — the same word as the MNP's "cakra-sū," the circle's birther).]*

**78** — pīṭhacakranikaraikadharmiṇī tvaṃ sthitā ca satataṃ samantataḥ | sadbhirudyamanirantarātmabhirlakṣyase'mba niravagrahodayā ||
> The one-natured (eka-dharmiṇī) with the seat-circle's host (pīṭha-cakra-nikara), you stand, always, on all sides; by the good, whose selves are the unceasing effort (udyama-nirantara), you are seen, O mother, of the unimpeded rising (niravagraha-udayā).
> *[The seat-circle's mother: one-natured with its host, standing everywhere; seen by the unceasingly-efforting good. The udaya (the rising) — the batch's structural word (the MNP's udayas) in the stotra's form.]*

**79** — īraṇena vividhena vīratāṃ yo'yamakṣaragaṇaḥ prapadyate | antyamīśamasi taṃ tridhāmakaṃ varṇaśaktipadavī ca citkalā ||
> This phoneme-host (akṣara-gaṇa) which attains the hero-ness (vīratā) by the manifold motion (īraṇa) — you are that, the last lord (antya-īśa), the three-stationed (tridhāmaka), the phoneme-power's path (varṇa-śakti-padavī), and the consciousness-phoneme (citkalā).
> *[The phoneme-host's hero: the letters' motion (īraṇa) as the attaining of the hero-ness; the goddess as the last lord (the antya — the h? the last phoneme), the three-stationed, the path of the phoneme-powers, the citkalā (the consciousness-phoneme — the same as the Yoginīhṛdaya 1/14's citkalā).]*

**80** — yā'nalārkaśaśivigrahā kalānāhatāmbaravisarpiṇī śive | pīṭhataḥ prabhṛti yāvadarcyate tāvadunmiṣati tanmayī satī ||
> She of the fire-sun-moon's body (anala-arka-śaśi-vigrahā), the kalā, flowing in the un-struck-sky (anāhata-ambara-visarpiṇī), O Śiva — as long as she is worshipped from the seat on — so long she flashes, being made of that (tanmayī).
> *[The fire-sun-moon kalā (the bindu-triad — the batch's shared agnīṣoma) flowing in the unstruck sky; the worship from the seat on as the flashing's condition. The report's visarga/bindu dossiers.]*

**81** — nirjanasthitilayaprathātmike'grāsanityaniravagrahodayaḥ | jṛmbhitastvadanupākṣadīpito dvyaṣṭakaṃ manurume ! tavāsanam ||
> Of the nature of the manifestation of the lonely-stay-and-dissolution (nirjana-sthiti-laya-prathā), the primeval, perpetual, unimpeded rising (agrāsa-nitya-niravagraha-udaya) — the expanded, lit by your near-gaze (anupākṣa-dīpita) — the sixteenfold (dvyaṣṭaka), O Umā's [lord?] [X], your seat (āsana).
> *[[X] — "manurume" (the vocative's reading) is the corrupt close; the force: the sixteenfold seat (the dvyaṣṭaka — the same sixteen as the moon's kalās), lit by the goddess's gaze. The report's visarga-dossier: the sixteen.]*

**82** — nityabhātaruci viśvaghasmaraṃ durnirīkṣamapi sanniketanam | tvaṃ śmaśānamasi vīrahṛdgataṃ kṣīṇavṛtti karavīramijyase ||
> Ever-shining (nitya-bhāta-ruci), the world-devourer (viśva-ghasmaram), hard-to-behold (durnirīkṣam), the near-abode (sanniketanam) — you are the burning-ground (śmaśāna), gone into the hero's heart (vīra-hṛdgata), of the diminished activity (kṣīṇa-vṛtti), worshipped as the karavīra.
> *[The burning-ground: the goddess as the world-devouring śmaśāna in the hero's heart — the MNP's caturtha-udaya's four śmaśānas (our T1) in the stotra's form; the "karavīra" — the MNP's own close-word ("outside that, the karavīra-form," the commentary). The batch's shared devouring-image.]*

**83** — viśvasaṃharaṇalīlayotkaṭaṃ vyomni śaśvaduditaṃ nirāśraye | kṣetrapālamanupādhikollasat tvadvimarśamabhinandatīśvare ||
> The terrible (utkaṭa) in the play of the world's retraction (viśva-saṃharaṇa-līlā), risen ever in the unsupported sky (nirāśraya-vyoman) — the field-lord (kṣetrapāla), flashing without condition (anupādhikollasat) — your reflexion (tvad-vimarśa), O Lord, [I] rejoice in.
> *[The field-lord (kṣetrapāla): the world's retraction's play, risen in the unsupported sky — the MNP's caturtha-udaya v. 4's kṣetrapāla (our T1, the field-body's protector) in the stotra's form. The reflexion (vimarśa) as the field-lord's own — the report's vimarśa-dossier.]*

**84** — khecarīprabhṛtiramba śākinīprānta eṣa tava yo gurukramaḥ | sṛṣṭitaśca yadanākhyavikramaṃ cakrametadakhilaṃ tavodgamaḥ ||
> Beginning with the khecarī, O mother, ending with the Śākinī — this, your guru-order (gurukrama); and from the creation, the un-nameable-stride (anākhya-vikrama) — this whole wheel (cakra), your rising (udgama).
> *[The guru-order: from the khecarī to the Śākinī — the goddesses' sequence (the same khecarī-to-śākinī arc as the MNP's vāmeśī-series, our T1's pañcama udaya); the creation-to-anākhya stride. The report's krama-dossier: the order as the transmission.]*

**85** — taddvayollasitamamba ! vartate yatra nityamiha sāmarasyataḥ | sanniketapadanityajṛmbhitollāsa eṣa tava me purassaraḥ ||
> That pair's flashing, O mother, which stands here eternally in the equal-taste (sāmarasyataḥ) — the expansion of the near-abode-station's perpetual expansion (sanniketapada-nitya-jṛmbhita-ullāsa) — this, of you, is my guide (purassara).
> *[The pair (the khecarī-śākinī range) in the equal-taste; the near-abode's expansion as the goddess's guide (purassara). The report's sāmarasya (the equal-taste) as the pair's ground.]*

**86** — bhogyabhoktṛrucighasmaraṃ manaḥ pañcavāhamayamucyate tamaḥ | nirniketapadameti yatra te ko'pyasau satatamadhvarotsavaḥ ||
> The mind (manaḥ), devouring the shine of the enjoyed-and-enjoyer (bhogya-bhoktṛ-ruci-ghasmaram), made of the five-power (pañca-vāha-maya), is called the darkness (tamas); where it attains your station-without-abode (nirniketa-pada) — there that one, the perpetual sacrifice-festival (adhvara-utsava).
> *[The mind's transfiguration: the five-power-made mind, the darkness, becomes the perpetual sacrifice when it attains the without-abode station. The pañca-vāha (the five-power) — the MNP's own term (our T1's pañcama udaya).]*

**87** — pūrṇasattvaraja-ādihṛdgatādhiṣṭhitā tvakhilalokavāhinī | pañcadhā vahasi śāśvatodayā siddhasindhuriva siddhasevitā ||
> Seated in the heart, with the fullness, the sattva, the rajas and the rest (pūrṇa-sattva-raja-ādi-hṛdgata), superintending (adhiṣṭhitā), the bearer of the whole world (akhila-loka-vāhinī), you bear fivefold (pañcadhā vahasi), of the eternal rising (śāśvatodayā), served by the accomplished as the accomplished-ocean (siddha-sindhu).
> *[The fivefold bearer: the goddess bearing the whole world fivefold from the heart, the eternal rising; the siddha-ocean image. The report's śakti-dossier: the power as the world's bearer.]*

**88** — vyomayānamatimatpurassaraḥ pañcadhā prasara eṣa tāvakaḥ | jṛmbhamāṇaguruvaktracitpathollāsimūrtirayamāsthito jagat ||
> The sky-vehicle (vyoma-yāna), the intellect's guide (mati-purassara), fivefold this your outflow (prasara); the flashing form in the guru's-speech's consciousness-path's expansion (guru-vaktra-cit-pathollāsi-mūrti) — this world stands [there].
> *[The sky-vehicle (the vyoma-yāna — the khecaratā's vehicle) and the fivefold outflow; the world standing in the guru's-speech's consciousness-path. The report's vāc-dossier: the speech as the world's path.]*

**89** — spandamādyamanu kurvatī śive nirniketanapathotthitā guṇaiḥ | śūnyadhāma vasasi tvamiṣṭitaḥ prāgadhiṣṭhitaravā vijṛmbhase ||
> Following the first pulse (spanda-ādyam anu), making, O Śiva, risen on the path-without-abode (nirniketa-pathotthitā) by the qualities (guṇaiḥ) — you dwell in the void-abode (śūnya-dhāma), O desired one, expanding with the earlier-supervised sound (prāg-adhiṣṭhita-ravā).
> *[The void-abode: the goddess following the first pulse, risen by the qualities, dwelling in the void-abode (the report's śūnya-dossier); the sound's supervision (the rāva — the MNP's rāva-krama). The spanda (the pulse) as her first step — the report's spanda-dossier.]*

**90** — ciccamatkaraṇarūḍhanirbharā tvaṃ purā sphurasi saiva khecarī | rūpasaṃgrahadarśitaiṣaṇā tejasā svarasavāhinā'mbike ||
> The consciousness's wonder-making (ciccamatkaraṇa), the full-mounting (rūḍha-nirbharā), you, first, flash — that very khecarī (the sky-goer), whose seeking is shown by the form's gathering (rūpa-saṃgraha-darśita-eṣaṇā), by the self-taste-flowing radiance (svarasa-vāhinā tejasā), O Ambikā.
> *[The khecarī: the consciousness's wonder (camatkāra — the rasa-term), the first flashing, the form-gathering. The report's khecarī-dossier: the sky-goer as the power, never the haṭha-gesture.]*

**91** — devi naikaviṣayodyamasphuṭārohiṇī bhavasi saiva bhūcarī | sparśasaṃgrahaṇanityasammukhī svairitāmajahatī svabhāvajām ||
> O Devī, the many-objects' effort, the clear-rising (naika-viṣaya-udyama-sphuṭārohiṇī) — you become that very bhūcarī (the earth-goer), ever facing the touch's gathering (sparśa-saṃgraha-nitya-sammukhī), not giving up the freedom (svairitām ajahatī) born of your own nature (svabhāva-jām).
> *[The bhūcarī: the many-objects' rising, the touch's gathering, holding the freedom (svairitā). The four goers begin — the report's khecarī/bhūcarī dossiers (the same tetrad as the MNP's vāmeśī-series).]*

**92** — pratyagātmani dṛśo'vakuñcanā viśvamannamudare vitanvatī | grasya saṃhṛtimukhena dikcarī saiva devi rasacarvaṇonmukhī ||
> In the inner self (pratyag-ātman), the sight's contraction (dṛśo'vakuñcanā), spreading the world as the food in the belly (viśvam annam udare), devouring (grasya), by the mouth of the retraction (saṃhṛti-mukha) — that very dikcarī (the direction-goer), O Devī, facing the taste's chewing (rasa-carvaṇa-unmukhī).
> *[The dikcarī: the sight's contraction, the world as the food devoured by the retraction's mouth — the batch's devouring-image (the Kālasaṃkarṣaṇī's consumption) in the goer's form.]*

**93** — prāptacittamukhato'dhitasthuṣī svasvarūpamaniketamañjasā | raudrabhāvakṛtabāhyajṛmbhaṇā gocarī tvamasi gandhahāriṇī ||
> Having attained the mind's mouth (prāpta-citta-mukha), standing in it (adhitasthuṣī), your own form, the without-abode (aniketa), directly (añjasā); the fierce-being-made outer-expansion (raudra-bhāva-kṛta-bāhya-jṛmbhaṇā) — you are the gocarī (the sense-field-goer), the fragrance-remover (gandha-hāriṇī).
> *[The gocarī: the mind's-mouth attainment, the without-abode form, the fierce expansion; the fragrance-remover (the smell-sense's power). The four goers complete — the sensory-cosmology of the Krama.]*

**94** — brahmavigrahakalādhirohiṇī pañcavāhavapuṣaḥ kalā tava | sāmarasyaghaṭitodayā śive śaśvadullasati siddhamaṇḍale ||
> The kalā mounting the Brahmā-body's [five] levels (brahma-vigraha-kalā-adhirohiṇī), the kalā of the five-power's body (pañca-vāha-vapus) — yours, the equal-taste-made rising (sāmarasya-ghaṭita-udayā), O Śiva, ever shines in the accomplished-circle (siddha-maṇḍala).
> *[The Brahmā-bodied kalā: the five-levels' mounting, the five-power's kalā, the equal-taste rising in the accomplished-circle. The report's kalā-dossier: the unit-energies.]*

**95** — bhānuśītakaravahnikuṇḍalībindusūtritaparāvarasthalā | rudraśaktiriha kālakarṣiṇī tvatkapañcavidhavṛttisūcinī ||
> The Rudra-power (rudraśakti) here, the Kālakarṣiṇī (the time-drawer), strung (sūtritā) [between] the sun, the moon, the fire, the kuṇḍalī, the bindu — the upper-lower stations (parāvara-sthalā) — the pointer of the fivefold activities (pañca-vidha-vṛtti-sūcinī).
> *[The Kālakarṣiṇī: the Rudra-power strung between the sun/moon/fire/kuṇḍalī/bindu — the batch's shared Kālasaṃkarṣaṇī (the time-drawer, the MNP's yāga-goddess, the Yoginīhṛdaya's cosmology); the pointer of the fivefold activities. The verse is the Krama's signature goddess in the stotra's voice.]*
